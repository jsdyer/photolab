Privacy and export details

Photo Lab reads selected EXIF tags and decodes pixels locally. The exported JPEG or PNG carries the new pixels without the original EXIF, GPS or camera metadata. JPEG fills transparent regions in white. Export dimensions are capped at 8,192 pixels per side and 24 megapixels. A JSON report may include sensitive GPS coordinates present in the original.
